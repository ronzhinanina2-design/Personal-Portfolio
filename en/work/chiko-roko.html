Chiko & Roko: NFT collectibles built from scratch

A full product design journey — from blank canvas to a living NFT platform used by nearly 100,000 collectors every day.

Year
2021–2022
Team
Founding product team · PM · Engineers · 2 designers
Surfaces
Web app · Mobile · AR
Tools
Figma · Miro · Photoshop · Illustrator · Notion

Smeshariki is one of Russia's biggest cartoon franchises. We turned its characters into NFT collectibles fans could collect, trade, and view in AR — starting at Expo 2020 Dubai and building it out over the next 14 months.

Collecting, trading, auctions, KYC, AR — over 500 screens, each one making complex logic feel effortless.

The product kept growing. Our job was to keep it feeling like one system — flexible enough that screen 500 belonged next to screen 1.

From 800 copies per drop to 50,000. Half a million collectors, 100+ contributing artists.
