The project

Digital Collectibles is a web platform born from the Chiko & Roko universe — part NFT marketplace, part community space. The idea was simple: collectors don't just want to own things, they want to belong somewhere.

I worked as a UI/UX Designer directly with the client's art director and dev team, taking the product from concept to 500+ approved screens and interactions.

The work

The core challenge was the social layer. Clubs, competitions, quizzes, chats, comments. Subscription-based features. Public and private profiles. Things most NFT platforms weren't thinking about in 2022.

I was responsible for designing all of it — across web and mobile. The mobile adaptation had its own logic: bottom navigation, full-screen experiences freed from browser chrome, and layouts rethought for thumb reach.

What I enjoyed most

The Artist page was my favourite part — each artist got their own shop and identity within the platform. Alongside that, designing the profile system (with its public/private logic and subscription tiers) was genuinely new territory for me. I liked that.

Design decisions

The community features needed to feel light and fun — not like a dashboard, not like a crypto app. Every screen had to feel like a place people would actually want to hang out in.

Outcome

500+ screens and interactions — designed, iterated, and signed off. A full social NFT platform ready to build.
