The project

The creators of Smeshariki — one of Russia's most beloved cartoons — wanted to turn two of their characters into NFT collectibles. Artists worldwide could put their own spin on the rabbit and hedgehog, and fans could collect, trade, and view them in AR. The first figure launched at Expo 2020 in Dubai. We started with a blank file.

Approach

We joined the founding team early and stayed for 14 months — from research and flows to shipped UI. As the product grew, so did the scope: KYC, onboarding, an auction system, AR. Each new feature meant going back to earlier decisions and making them hold up.

The work

500+ screens across collecting, trading, and viewing. The auction flow gave collectors a real reason to compete. KYC had to feel easy for fans while actually being compliant. And the 3D/AR viewer had to make a digital toy feel worth owning.

Design decisions

The hard part wasn't any one screen — it was keeping 500 screens coherent as the product kept changing. We built systems flexible enough to grow without losing the thread.

Outcome

Edition sizes went from 800 to 50,000. The platform reached half a million active collectors. Over a hundred artists joined from around the world. Not bad for something that started as a blank Figma file.
