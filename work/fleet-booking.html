FIT FLEET: Fewer Steps, Faster Bookings.

A redesign of the appointment flow for FIT FLEET's management platform — simplifying every step until clients said it just made sense.

The problem

Scheduling a car for service should take a minute. For FIT FLEET's clients, it took much longer — the booking flow was cluttered, front-loaded, and gave users no real sense of where they were or what came next.

Approach

We walked the existing flow end to end and mapped every friction point in Miro. Then I restructured it around one idea: show only what the user needs right now, and make progress impossible to miss. We tested the redesign as an interactive Figma prototype before a single line of code was written.

The flow

The new booking experience breaks the process into focused, sequential steps — one clear task per screen. Instead of presenting all fields upfront, the flow guides users through vehicle selection, station choice, date and time, and confirmation as distinct moments. A persistent progress indicator keeps users oriented throughout. Each step is reversible, and the final confirmation screen summarizes everything before submission — so users can catch errors without starting over.

Design decisions

The biggest change wasn't visual — it was structural. Separating each stage meant users could focus on one decision at a time. Clean hierarchy and consistent inputs did the rest.

Outcome

Clients noticed immediately during prototype testing — the flow felt faster, cleaner, and easier to trust. That reaction, before a single line of code, was the clearest signal we got.
