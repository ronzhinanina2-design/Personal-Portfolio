FIT FLEET: Service quality platform

Most post-service feedback goes unfilled. We designed a structured assessment flow that corporate fleet clients would actually complete — yes/no questions by category, optional detail for those who want it, and nothing more.

The platform rolled out to several thousand corporate clients across desktop and mobile.

The problem

Fleet service clients had no structured way to share feedback after repairs. No consistent data, no quality signal, no way to act on what clients actually experienced.

Post-service surveys get abandoned. The form had to feel effortless — or it wouldn't get filled.

Approach

We mapped the service journey and identified where feedback was expected but never given. User stories helped define what clients needed — and what would make them drop off.

One principle guided the work: ask only what you can act on. Every question needed a clear owner on the business side, or it got cut.

The work

The assessment fits into two modal screens, with questions grouped by category — budget, speed, cost justification on the first; communication and repair quality on the second.

A separate optional step offers a free-text field. Clients who want to say more can. Everyone else just taps through.

Design decisions

Two screens — not one question per screen — gives users a sense of scope upfront. Category headers (Budget, Speed, Quality, Communication) break up the form and keep it from feeling like a wall of questions.

Every answer is binary. Yes or no. The free-form step is fully separate, so it never blocks clients who just want to submit.

Outcome

The platform launched as part of the FIT FLEET ecosystem and was implemented for several thousand corporate fleet clients — giving the business its first structured quality signal at scale.
